- [ ] Obtain executed distribution agreement and commission schedule
- [ ] Obtain permission to publish names, logos, policy wording and product information
- [ ] Request approved product feed, API or partner portal and sandbox documentation
- [ ] Confirm quote eligibility, premium collection, official receipt and policy issuance flow
- [ ] Confirm data processing terms, privacy/security and complaint escalation
- [ ] Complete integration and security testing before enabling checkout
